What the map shows

Heat stored in water warmer than 26 °C, from the surface down to the 26 °C isotherm (D26), computed from the model's temperature and salinity. The white contour marks 50 kJ/cm², a common threshold for cyclone intensification.

    About the data

    Student prototype by Team ByteForge for Smart India Hackathon 2026, problem statement 26067 (problem owner: INCOIS). Not affiliated with or endorsed by INCOIS or the Ministry of Earth Sciences. Not for operational use.

    Real data: Cyclone Amphan, May 2020

    Model
    HYCOM + NCODA global analysis (GOFS 3.1), analyses at 12:00 UTC, three days apart, from to . Sampled every 0.24° and averaged into 0.5° cells on 11 levels from 0 to 1000 m (75 m and 750 m interpolated between model levels). Temperature, salinity (PSS-78) and currents.
    Argo
    profiles from floats, from the Argo GDAC via Ifremer ERDDAP. Delayed-mode or adjusted values where they exist; only QC flags 1, 2, 5 and 8 kept.
    BGC-Argo
    Chlorophyll-a (mg/m³) and dissolved oxygen (µmol/kg) from profiles of biogeochemical floats, from the synthetic BGC-Argo profiles on Ifremer ERDDAP. Adjusted values only, never raw real-time values, with QC flags 1, 2, 5 or 8 and a range check (chlorophyll −0.1 to 50 mg/m³, oxygen −5 to 600 µmol/kg).
    Cyclone
    Six-hourly positions and winds from IBTrACS v04r01: IMD (RSMC New Delhi, 3-minute winds) and JTWC (1-minute winds), shown side by side.
    Coast and sea floor
    ETOPO1, sampled every 0.1° (now superseded by ETOPO 2022).

    Read with care

    • HYCOM assimilates Argo, so the model–Argo comparison is a consistency check, not independent validation.
    • The model is shown on 11 levels and 0.5° cells; features smaller than that are smoothed out.
    • Heat potential uses EOS-80 density and cp = 4000 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹; some operational products use 4178, about 4.5 % higher.
    • Depth is stretched about times relative to distance, and the sea floor below 1000 m is compressed a further times.
    • Current labels mark where these currents usually flow; they are not drawn from this data.
    • Current streaks move at an illustrative speed, not a physical one.
    • HYCOM has no chlorophyll or oxygen. In real mode those come only from BGC-Argo floats, so there is no model column to compare them with.
    • No glider data is included for this period.

    Add your own data

    Add data (or a file dropped on the 3D view) reads CSV or tab-separated text and NetCDF-3 files: Argo profile files, Ifremer EGO glider files, ERDDAP tabledap downloads and CF profile data. Profiles appear as markers, follow the timeline (within 1.5 days of each analysis, unless you show all times) and open in the profile panel against the model column. Files are read in this browser and never uploaded. NetCDF-4 files need converting first, with the data pipeline or by exporting NetCDF-3 or CSV.

    The samples/ folder next to this file has three to try: a real BGC-Argo profile from May 2020 as NetCDF-3 from Ifremer ERDDAP, real Argo temperature and salinity samples from this snapshot as CSV, and a made-up CTD cast for testing, which is not real data. samples/README.md says which is which.

    Synthetic demo

    Analytic fields, floats and gliders generated in the browser to show the tools on data with known behaviour, including a float with a deliberate bad salinity sample that the density-inversion check flags. It is not a real ocean state; instruments are labelled SYN-F01 onwards and SYN-G1 onwards, and times are demo days, not dates.

    Files it reads

    • Comma-, tab- or semicolon-separated text with one row per sample: latitude, longitude, time, depth or pressure, and any of temperature, salinity, chlorophyll, oxygen and current (eastward and northward). Surface currents need no depth.
    • NetCDF-3 (.nc): Argo profile files, Ifremer EGO glider files, ERDDAP tabledap downloads, CF profile data.

    NetCDF-4 files need converting first: run them through the data pipeline, or export them as NetCDF-3 or CSV.

    Match the columns

    Some column names weren't recognised. Pick the column that holds each quantity. Latitude, longitude and time are required, and depth or pressure unless the file holds surface currents (eastward and northward).
